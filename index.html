Dabao Uploader

Dabao Uploader is a private, internal command-line tool used only by the Dabao team to publish videos we produce ourselves to our own four YouTube channels:

  • AI壹号 (@dbai001)
  • 大宝说创业 (@dbcy01)
  • DBAI777 (@dbai777)
  • 健康安心 (@jiankanganxin)
YouTube This tool uses YouTube API Services.

What it does

After a video is finished and approved by the channel owner, the tool uploads the video file, sets its title, description, tags and custom thumbnail, uploads our own subtitle file, adds the video to one of our own playlists, and schedules the publish time. It does not access, download, analyze or display any other channel's content, and it has no outside users.
